About

I trained as a microbiologist — a B.S. in Biology from UC Riverside — and spent my days running endotoxin assays, bioburden testing, and environmental monitoring in GMP cleanrooms for medical device manufacturing. I care about data integrity, clean documentation, and closing the gap between the wet lab and the terminal.
